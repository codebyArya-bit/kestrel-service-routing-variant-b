# Decisions and limitations
Word and character features provide a compact local classifier. IDs, dates, outcomes and operational identifiers are excluded from inference features. Development selects the candidate; holdout is explanatory only after freeze. The employee can override the suggested team. Model support is uncalibrated; the keyword guard cannot detect every ambiguous or multi-intent request. Production retirement requires an adjudicated supervised pilot, measured review costs and an operational rollback. Display aliases preserve canonical submission names. Serving explanations are separate from training/scoring decisions.
